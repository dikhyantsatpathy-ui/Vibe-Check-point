# NO-CAP (SIH26188) — Ground-Truth Field Capture Protocol

## 1. Objective
Establish an empirical, privacy-compliant benchmark protocol to evaluate card boundary detection (`isolate_document_card`), OCR field extraction (`extract_fields`), and biometric verification under real-world checkpoint conditions (harsh lighting, desk shadows, physical tilt, and lens distortions).

---

## 2. Document Classes & Sample Size Targets
Evaluation requires a minimum target of **50 specimens per class** (total: 350 physical specimens):

1. **Aadhaar Card** (Physical PVC / laminated paper format)
2. **PAN Card** (NSDL / UTIITSL format)
3. **Voter ID (EPIC)** (Old card + new PVC format)
4. **Driving Licence** (Smart card Form 7 format)
5. **Passport (Republic of India)** (36-page bio booklet, TD3 MRZ)
6. **Nepali Citizenship Certificate / Passport** (Indo-Nepal bilateral treaty travel format)
7. **Bhutan Travel Permit / Identity Card** (Jaigaon / Phuentsholing border format)

---

## 3. Capture Conditions & Stratification Matrix

### 3.1 Device Tiers
Each specimen must be photographed across three distinct optical sensor tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Budget Android):** Entry-level smartphone sensor (e.g., 12MP fixed/basic autofocus, high compression, aggressive noise reduction).
- **Tier 2 (Mid-Range Android):** Standard modern device (e.g., 48MP/64MP quad-binned sensor, moderate sharpening).
- **Tier 3 (Flagship / iOS):** Premium camera system (e.g., iPhone 13+ or Pixel 6+, wide dynamic range, high optical resolution).

### 3.2 Perspective & Angle Variations
Each specimen is captured across 3 calibrated angle configurations:
- **Orthogonal (0°):** Flat on desk surface, camera perpendicular to card plane.
- **Moderate Tilt (15° Yaw / Pitch):** Realistic officer desk inspection tilt.
- **Extreme Skew (30° Yaw / Pitch):** Corner-held handheld border lane capture.

### 3.3 Illumination & Glare Regimes
- **Diffuse Desk Light:** Standard office/inspection booth fluorescent or LED panel lighting (~400–600 lux).
- **Direct Overhead Glare:** Concentrated top-down beam causing localized specular reflection on glossy laminate/hologram.
- **Backlit / High Contrast:** Window or background sunlight facing the lens, casting document in harsh shadow.

---

## 4. Privacy & Compliance Mandate (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Zero-Raw-Storage invariant:

1. **Preferred Method (Zero Compliance Risk):**
   - High-fidelity synthetic document layouts generated by `training/synth_fields.py` and `training/synth_mrz.py` printed at 1:1 scale on 300 GSM cardstock / PVC blanks.
   - Captured in physical space using real cameras. This guarantees **100% absence of real Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** while providing identical physical optical challenges (glare, paper texture, optical blur, tilt).

2. **Volunteer Real Specimens (If Acquired):**
   - Must undergo pre-ingestion redaction before committing to benchmark dataset.
   - PII fields (Name, Full ID number, Father/Spouse name, Residential address) must be irreversibly masked/blurred.
   - Only document boundary lines, security pattern backgrounds, and format headers may remain visible.
   - Signed volunteer consent logs must be maintained offline.

---

## 5. Evaluation Metrics & Success Gates

When run through `eval/field_capture/eval_field_robustness.py`:

- **Card Detection (IoU):** Intersection-over-Union against polygon/bounding annotations:
  - Orthogonal (0°): $\ge 0.90$ mIoU
  - Moderate tilt (15°): $\ge 0.85$ mIoU
  - Extreme skew (30°): $\ge 0.75$ mIoU
- **Field Extraction Precision & Recall:**
  - Mandatory ID numbers (Aadhaar 12-digit, PAN 10-char, DL number): $\ge 92\%$ recall across Tier 1–3 devices.
  - MRZ line character accuracy: $\ge 98\%$ character recall on Passports.
- **Degraded Mode Guard:**
  - If card boundary detection confidence falls below threshold (`CONF_THRES_CARD=0.35`), pipeline must tag `is_fallback: True` and route strictly to `REVIEW` (never `CLEAR`).
